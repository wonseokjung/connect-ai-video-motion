Start

Three steps. No install.

Node.js 18 or newer. No Python. With a coding agent (Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity), install the skill once and ask it to put motion graphics on your video.

More you can make

__N__ graphics, one line each.

Each clip is one line of plan.json on the author's own footage. The words on screen are words he said (Korean, shown in English). Copy a prompt and give it to your coding agent.

The kit is MIT. It runs on Remotion, which is free for individuals, companies with up to 3 employees and non-profits; other companies need a Remotion Company License. HyperFrames blocks are Apache-2.0, GSAP uses GreenSock's standard no-charge license. Every card shows the licence of its own source.

How it places things

It looks at you first.

01

Measure

Your face, your body joints and 21 points per hand, 15 times a second, plus the area you take up in the frame. On a Mac this needs nothing extra.

02

Place

Every graphic goes to empty space beside you and stays there while it is on screen. If there is no room it is hidden and the report says why. It is never squeezed over you.

03

Time

The plan is a table of "said word, graphic". A graphic lands 40 ms after its word. Text on screen is 1 to 4 of your own words.

04

Check

Before rendering, every problem is written as a sentence: covers the face, touches a hand, off the frame, low contrast, the same design twice.
